
Bull Analyst: **Bull Case for AAPL — July 31, 2026**

Let’s cut through the noise. Yes, AAPL dropped 8.6% today on record volume. That’s scary if you ignore *why* the stock ran 23% in five weeks. This is a healthy shakeout after a parabolic move, not a thesis breaker.

**The Bear’s Best Shot is a One-Time Item — and It’s Still a Blowout**

The bear will say margins are flattered by a 2-point tariff refund. Fine. Strip it out: gross margin is still ~48%, EPS is ~$1.91, and revenue grew **16% YoY to $109.4B**. That’s not a company slowing down — that’s Apple’s fastest growth in years. The tariff refund is a bonus, not the story. The story is iPhone demand and services resilience in a tough consumer environment.

**Technicals Say “Oversold,” Not “Broken”**

Today’s close at $308.91 sits just above the Bollinger lower band ($304.22) and essentially at the VWMA ($308.54). That means the selloff brought price back to the average cost of recent buyers — a natural support zone. RSI at 45 is neutral, not overbought, and not yet oversold. The MACD crossover is early and shallow. Support at $300 held today on the highest volume in the dataset. If that was distribution, it failed to break the round number.

**The Bear’s “Distribution” Narrative Ignores the Catalyst**

Record volume on a down day after a massive earnings beat? That’s profit-taking, not institutional exit. The Q3 beat (+29% EPS) was a *positive* catalyst. The market got ahead of itself at $344, and now it’s resetting. The 10 EMA at $327 is resistance, but the stock is only 6% below it — a reclaim is plausible within days if the $300 support holds.

**Valuation and Fundamentals Support the Long Game**

Apple’s dividend is stable at $0.27. The company is generating massive free cash flow, and services revenue continues to compound. The bear will point to the one-time tariff benefit as a reason to discount the quarter — but they’ll ignore that Apple’s underlying business grew 16% *organically*. That’s the kind of growth that justifies a premium multiple.

**The Debate**

Bear: “The MACD is bearish and RSI has room to fall.”
Bull: RSI at 45 is *neutral* — it’s not signaling a crash. And MACD crossovers after a 23% run are common. The real signal is price holding $300 on record volume. That’s buyers stepping in.

Bear: “The tariff refund is non-recurring.”
Bull: Correct — and that’s why the stock pulled back. But the market is now pricing in normalized margins (~48%), which are still excellent. The beat was so strong that even ex-benefit EPS grew ~20% YoY.

**Verdict**

This is a pullback in an uptrend, not a reversal. The fundamentals are accelerating, the balance sheet is fortress-like, and the technical setup is approaching oversold. I’m not just holding — I’m watching $300 as a potential accumulation zone. The bull case is intact.
